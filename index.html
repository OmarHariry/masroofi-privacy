Masroofi Privacy Policy

App: Masroofi (Android package com.masroofi.mobile) · Version covered: 1.0.0 and later · Last updated: 13 November 2026

1. Who we are

Masroofi is a personal expense and installment tracker. It is provided by Omar Hariry (“we”, “us”). Contact: masroofi.support@gmail.com (see section 12 for what happens if you write to us).

2. Summary

  • The financial data you enter or import in the app (transactions, installment plans, categories and the figures calculated from them) is stored and processed on your phone, in the app’s private storage. Masroofi has no account and no server of ours, and the app does not upload this data to us or to any other server.
  • Your financial data leaves your phone only if you choose to send it: when you export an encrypted backup and then save or send the file somewhere you pick (for example Google Drive, email or another phone). From that point the service you picked handles the file under its own policy (section 5).
  • Through the app, we do not collect, receive, sell or share your personal or financial data. The app contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting libraries. The only information we ever receive is what you choose to send us yourself, for example if you write to the support email address (section 12).
  • The “Insights” screens (monthly overview, history, spending by category, highlights, upcoming installments) are calculated on your phone from the entries you made. They use no artificial-intelligence service and no network connection (section 4).
  • You can export an encrypted backup and choose where the file goes. Only your password can open it, and nobody, including us, can recover it for you.
  • The app has an optional bank SMS import. It is off by default and does nothing until you read a consent screen and tap “Agree and continue”. It receives only new messages from the one sender you choose, processes them on your phone, and never uploads them (section 6).
  • Masroofi does not ask for contacts, location, camera, microphone or photo access, and it cannot send SMS.

4. Network use, third-party code and SDKs

  • The app’s own code does not send your financial data over the internet, and it has no sign-in, cloud sync or remote storage. Android lists the INTERNET permission for the app framework; the app does not use it to transmit your financial data.
  • Insights are calculated locally. Totals, comparisons, highlights and upcoming-installment figures are worked out on your phone with fixed arithmetic rules from the entries stored on it. They use no artificial-intelligence or machine-learning service, send nothing over the network, and are not stored apart from your entries.
  • The app is built with open-source libraries, namely Expo and React Native modules for local storage (SQLite), file access, file sharing, file picking and cryptography. These run on your device. We include no advertising, analytics, attribution, tracking or crash-reporting SDK.
  • The “Privacy policy” button in Settings opens this page in your web browser. That visit is handled by your browser and by the website host (GitHub Pages), which may log standard request information such as your IP address under its own privacy policy. We do not receive or process that information.

5. Encrypted backups you create

  • In Settings you can choose Export encrypted backup. The file contains your categories, transactions (including ones you deleted, see section 8), installment plans and payments, the links between paid installments (and down payments) and the expenses that record them, and import fingerprints. It does not contain pasted bank message text or SMS text. Backups made by earlier versions of the app can still be restored; they simply contain no installment-expense links.
  • The file is encrypted on your phone with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from your password with Argon2id. Passwords must be at least 12 characters.
  • We cannot recover a forgotten password and cannot open your backup.
  • Three different things happen, and only the first two are done by Masroofi. (1) On your phone: the app creates the encrypted file in its private storage. (2) Your choice: the app opens Android’s share sheet and you decide whether and where the file goes (for example Files, Google Drive, email or another phone). Masroofi does not choose the destination and does not upload the file itself. (3) Third parties you chose: if you pick a service such as Google Drive or an email app, that app or service receives and stores the encrypted file under its own terms and privacy policy, and may see information such as the file name, size, time and your account with it. We cannot see where the file went, and we cannot delete copies held by those services.
  • A temporary encrypted copy stays in the app’s private cache and is removed automatically the next time the app starts after it is more than 24 hours old. Restoring a backup replaces the current data on your phone after you confirm.
  • Android’s automatic cloud backup and phone-to-phone transfer are switched off for Masroofi, so your data is not copied to Google’s backup service automatically. This means that if you uninstall the app, lose your phone or change phones without an exported backup, your data cannot be recovered.

6. Optional bank SMS import (Android)

This feature is optional and off by default. Masroofi never receives a text message until you turn it on.

  • Consent first. When you choose Review or Auto-import, Masroofi shows a screen that explains what it will receive, why and how it is used, with the buttons “Agree and continue” and “No thanks”. Going back or closing it counts as “No thanks”, and nothing is requested from Android. Auto-import needs a second, separate agreement. Only after you agree does Android show its own permission prompt.
  • What it can access. The RECEIVE_SMS permission lets Masroofi receive new text messages as they arrive. It does not read your inbox or old messages and cannot send messages. Android words this permission as “send and view SMS messages”; the app tells you so before asking.
  • Only one sender. You type the one sender you want to accept (for example the name shown above your bank’s messages, a short number or a phone number). The app accepts one sender at a time: saving a different sender replaces the previous one. Messages from any other sender are dropped without the app reading, keeping or processing their text; only a counter changes. When SMS import is off, no incoming message is looked at at all.
  • What happens to a message from your sender.
    1. One-time codes, passwords, security and fraud-alert messages are recognised and dropped. They are never stored.
    2. Other messages are kept on your phone, encrypted with an Android Keystore key that the app cannot export, so they can be processed or reviewed. A waiting message expires 24 hours after it arrives and is never processed or shown after that. Expired messages are deleted when the app next checks its message queue, which happens when you open the Home or Settings screen or when another message from your sender arrives. If you do not open the app and no new message arrives, the expired encrypted text can therefore stay on your phone for longer than 24 hours, without being used. The sender and the time of receipt of a waiting message are kept in the app’s private storage unencrypted; the text is encrypted.
    3. In Review mode nothing is saved until you open the message and confirm it.
    4. In Auto-import mode (experimental and optional; it is off unless you turn it on and agree on its own screen, which says that it can make mistakes) a message that the app recognises with high confidence as a completed card purchase is saved as a transaction without you opening it: a stated debit, one amount in your default currency (the currency chosen in Settings; it must be one of EGP, SAR, AED, USD, EUR, GBP, KWD or QAR), a merchant, an unambiguous date, and no duplicate. It is marked “Auto-imported from bank SMS” and you can edit, delete or undo it (an undone message is not imported again). Transfers, refunds, pending or failed payments, security messages, unclear or doubtful messages, possible duplicates and unknown or ambiguous currencies are never saved automatically: they wait for your review or are discarded. Auto-import can be wrong (a wrong amount, shop, date or category, or a fake message sent from a forged sender name), so every automatic transaction stays visible and correctable. A suggested category can occasionally be wrong; unknown shops are saved under “Other”. You can switch back to Review or Off at any time; transactions already saved stay. Everything is decided on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere.
    5. After a message is saved or discarded, its text is deleted straight away; an expired message is deleted as described above. When a message expires before you have reviewed it, the app may keep a short notice with only the sender name and the times the message arrived and expired, so it can tell you that a message was missed; the text is not kept, and you can dismiss these notices in the app. A transaction saved from a message contains only the amount, merchant, date and category, and a one-way fingerprint used to avoid importing the same message twice. The message text is not kept in the database.
  • Messages in another currency. A message whose currency differs from your default currency (or when your default currency cannot be determined) is never saved automatically. It waits for your review, where the app shows the original amount from the message (for example 165.50 AED) and leaves the amount you will save empty for you to enter. The app never converts between currencies and never assumes an exchange rate.
  • Messages that match installment payments. If a message looks like a duplicate of an expense that already records an installment payment, it is held for your review as a possible duplicate and nothing is saved. If you choose “It’s the installment payment: don’t add it again”, the app stores only a one-way fingerprint of the message that points to that existing expense, so the same message is not imported again. No new transaction is created, the expense is not changed, and the message text is not stored. You can instead choose to save it as a separate transaction.
  • The message text stays on your phone. The text is processed by the app on the device only. The app does not upload, share or sell it, it is not used for advertising, and it is not used for anything other than adding the transactions you see in the app. It is not included in backups. A transaction you save from a message is an ordinary entry (amount, merchant, date, category); like any entry, it is part of a backup only if you create one and choose to send it somewhere (section 5).
  • You stay in control. Choose Turn off SMS import in Settings at any time (this also deletes messages still waiting), remove the sender, or take the permission away in Android Settings → Apps → Masroofi → Permissions.
  • Limits. The reader is built for specific English and Arabic bank message formats and for amounts in the eight supported currencies (EGP, SAR, AED, USD, EUR, GBP, KWD, QAR), which it never converts; messages it does not recognise, or whose currency is unclear, are not saved automatically. There is no accuracy guarantee: it has not been measured on real bank messages. Sender names are not verified by the phone network, so someone else could send a message that looks like it came from your bank; Review mode is the recommended way to start, and Auto-import is experimental.
  • Devices without telephony. On tablets and other devices that cannot receive text messages, the feature shows as unavailable and nothing is requested.

7. Permissions

The Android build lists these permissions: INTERNET (see section 4), VIBRATE (haptic feedback), WAKE_LOCK (lets the background step finish processing a message you chose to receive; granted automatically) and RECEIVE_SMS. RECEIVE_SMS is the only one that shows a prompt, and only after you agree on the consent screen described in section 6; if you never turn on SMS import you are never asked. The app does not request READ_SMS, SEND_SMS, call log, contacts, location, camera, microphone, photo or storage permissions. Files you pick for restore are chosen through Android’s system file picker, which gives the app access to only the file you select.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Your financial data stays on your phone until you remove it. Uninstalling Masroofi, or clearing its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Masroofi → Storage, permanently deletes all data held by the app. We cannot delete it for you because we never receive it.
  • Undoing an installment payment that the app recorded as an expense removes that expense from your totals (it is marked deleted as described next); an expense you had entered yourself and linked to a payment is only unlinked and stays. Deleting an installment plan keeps the expenses recorded for its paid payments as ordinary expenses by default.
  • Deleting a transaction or installment plan inside the app hides it and marks it deleted; the entry remains in the app’s private database and in later encrypted backups so a restore cannot bring back removed items by mistake. The app currently has no “erase all data” button; to remove everything, use the uninstall or clear-storage method above.
  • Bank SMS text is removed from the device when the message is saved or discarded, when you choose Turn off SMS import, or when you uninstall the app. A message that is not reviewed expires 24 hours after it arrives and its text is deleted the next time the app checks its message queue (section 6).
  • Backup files you exported are under your control; delete them yourself from wherever you saved or sent them.
  • Because we hold no copy of the financial data kept in the app, there is nothing for us to access, correct, port or erase on request. You can do all of this in the app or by uninstalling. Messages you send to the support email address are covered in section 12.

9. Security

Your data is protected by your phone’s own security (screen lock and Android app sandboxing). The on-device database is not separately encrypted; exported backups are, as described above. The text of waiting bank SMS messages is encrypted with an Android Keystore key; a message expires 24 hours after it arrives and is deleted when the app next checks its queue (section 6). No method is perfectly secure; keep your device locked and your backup password safe.

10. Children

Masroofi is a general-purpose finance tool and is not directed at children under 13. The app collects no data from anyone, including children, and we do not knowingly receive personal information from children, including by email.

11. Changes to this policy

If the app’s data behaviour changes (for example if a future version uses the network or processes data in a new way), we will update this page and its date before that version is released.
